THE PROIECT ROMÂNIA COMMUNITY

Projects grow
through the people who contribute.

We are looking for collaborators and contributors in Romania and abroad. Bring your experience, constructive criticism, useful connections or time for a specific task.

Sport and education

Coaches, teachers, parents and club staff: help us assess whether the proposals meet real needs on the ground.

Research and expertise

Contribute data, cost validation, analysis, safeguarding knowledge or public administration experience.

Communication and technology

Help with writing, translation, visual materials, platform development and explaining the projects.

Communities and partnerships

Connect us with schools, clubs, organisations, diaspora communities or potential pilot partners.

Start with a conversation.

Tell us how you would like to contribute and how much time you can offer. We will then discuss an initial task and expectations on both sides.

We aim to build a community based on respect, evidence, transparency and recognition of contributions. Constructive criticism, with explanations or alternatives, is welcome.

The community is taking shape. This is an invitation to volunteer, not an offer of paid employment. The terms of any collaboration will be discussed before work begins.
