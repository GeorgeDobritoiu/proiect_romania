Minimum project standard

  1. Problem and evidence

    Explain the problem, who is affected and the evidence behind it. Include verifiable sources and access dates.

  2. Proposal

    Describe the solution, what it changes and how it connects with existing structures.

  3. Pilot

    Start small: location, duration, participants and conditions for expansion.

  4. Estimated budget

    Separate costs, assumptions, available resources and potential funding from confirmed funding.

  5. Indicators

    Define the baseline, intended outcomes and how they will be measured.

  6. Risks

    Set out risks, dependencies and mitigation measures.

  7. Responsibilities

    Identify who coordinates, delivers and assesses results.

  8. Conflicts of interest

    Declare the author’s relevant professional, financial and organisational interests.
