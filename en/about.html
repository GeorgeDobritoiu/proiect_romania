ABOUT THE PLATFORM

A place for constructive proposals.

Proiect România is an initiative by George Dobritoiu, open to contributions from people with different areas of expertise.

What brings us together

We aim to make public discussion more useful through documented problems, clear proposals and implementation plans. Each project keeps its own identity and author.

Independence and integrity

The author of a strategy and companies associated with them do not bid to implement that strategy. Relevant interests are declared with the proposal.

Dialogue with institutions is part of developing workable proposals. A proposal does not imply an institutional partnership or endorsement.
