EXISTING INITIATIVE

Ajungem MARI — educational support

Consistent support for children and young people in care.

Asociația Lindenfeld

Editorial presentation of an existing initiative. The programme belongs to its organiser; no partnership is announced.

The organiser

Ajungem MARI is an Asociația Lindenfeld programme supporting the education of children and young people in care and disadvantaged circumstances. It includes volunteering and developmental support activities.

Checked on 10 October 2026: the organiser states that volunteer recruitment has closed and selection has started. This page does not advertise open applications.

Ways to help

  • Follow the official website for the next volunteer campaign and read its commitment requirements.
  • Explore donation and support options published by the association.
  • Businesses can discuss needs and collaboration directly with the team.

Before volunteering — our recommendation

Assess the time you can consistently give, including travel. Ask about selection, training, supervision and reporting concerns. Work within the organiser's procedures.

Protect children's identities and personal stories. Avoid publishing identifiable details or photographs and arranging direct contact outside the programme.

What we promote

Patient, consistent support. Share the original information and refer interested people to the organiser for current conditions.
