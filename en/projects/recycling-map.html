EXISTING INITIATIVE

Harta Reciclării — Recycling Map

Find collection points and contribute useful local information.

Viitor Plus

Editorial presentation of an existing initiative. The programme belongs to its organiser; no partnership is announced.

A practical community tool

Harta Reciclării is a Viitor Plus programme. Its platform lists collection points and accepted materials. The organisers also provide educational resources and volunteering opportunities, including contributions of new locations.

How to use it

  1. Open the map and search your area and material type.
  2. Confirm opening hours and acceptance conditions before travelling; local information can change.
  3. Report an error or new location through the platform's channels.

A local activity — our suggestion

An adult team can verify five neighbourhood locations, recording addresses, opening times, accepted materials and the verification date. Submit updates to the organisers and share a local guide linking to the map.

Schools can use the educational resources to discuss collection habits. Do not organise improvised handling of hazardous waste.

What to measure

Count verified locations and corrected entries. Do not report quantities recycled without confirmed measurement.
