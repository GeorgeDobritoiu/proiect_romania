IVF in Romania: funding and access

Public programmes, eligibility checks and support for people facing infertility.

Proiect România · ghid de orientare

Editorial presentation of an existing initiative. The programme belongs to its organiser; no partnership is announced.
Editorial check: 1 November 2026. The national 2026–2030 framework is approved. Remaining places and funding have not been verified in real time. Confirm the current application session directly with the organiser.
Important: the 2026 national application session closes on 15 November 2026. This deadline does not guarantee remaining places or funding.

National programme, 2026–2030

The Ministry of Labour, Family, Youth and Social Solidarity administers the programme under Government Decision 264/2026 and Ministerial Order 684/2026. Support totals up to RON 15,000: RON 5,000 for medication and RON 10,000 for procedures, through digital vouchers.

Government Decision No. 264 of 23 April 2026 was published in Official Gazette No. 328 on 24 April 2026; Ministerial Order No. 684 of 29 May 2026 was published in Official Gazette No. 469 on 4 June 2026. The support is tax-exempt and may be awarded up to three times to the same beneficiary during 2026–2030. Funding is envisaged for up to 10,000 beneficiaries annually, subject to approved budget allocations.

Main eligibility checks

  • Married or unmarried couples, or single women, with a medical indication for assisted reproduction.
  • The woman must be aged 24–42 inclusive when applying.
  • At least one partner, or the single applicant, must be a Romanian citizen domiciled in Romania.
  • Both partners, or the single applicant, must be insured within Romania's health insurance system.
  • Procedures take place at a participating medical provider in Romania.

This is a summary; the organiser determines eligibility from the complete application. Under the implementing rules, applicants who have received financial support during the current year through a similar programme run by another public authority or institution are not eligible to apply. If you only submitted another application, ask the organiser for clarification.

Application steps

  1. Find the current announcement and participating providers on the ministry website.
  2. Discuss the medical indication, documents and costs with a specialist at a participating clinic.
  3. Submit the national application exclusively online via up-comunitate.ro, after checking the link on the ministry website.
  4. Prepare signed forms, the declaration, data agreement, identity copies, IVF indication and insurance evidence required by the implementing rules.
  5. Keep your registration reference and monitor messages and deadlines.

The rules set 15 November as the annual submission deadline. This does not confirm available places: approvals depend on eligibility, complete-file order and funding. The IVF medical indication (Annex 1b) must be issued by an obstetrics and gynaecology specialist qualified in infertility treatment and assisted reproduction, employed by or collaborating with a participating clinic, no more than 60 days before upload and not before the clinic joined the programme.

Previous-edition beneficiaries may reapply only after the previous procedure has been fully settled. An application marked “NOU” (“NEW”) can be corrected; once evaluation begins, requested clarifications must be supplied within 10 calendar days of notification. Complete applications are assessed chronologically.

Ask for a written cost breakdown

Check voucher coverage, additional charges, cancellation or postponement terms and voucher deadlines. Applicants pay costs above voucher values. The rules exclude reimbursement for treatment or procedures performed before vouchers are granted.

Funding does not guarantee full cost coverage or a medical outcome. A specialist must determine the appropriate treatment.

Preliminary investigations, embryo genetic testing and cryopreservation are expressly excluded from reimbursement. Withdrawal for personal reasons or failure to use vouchers may result in repayment, with interest and penalties under the applicable rules and contract.

Local example: Bucharest Sector 2

DGASPC Sector 2 announced a 2026 session starting on 16 September, with up to RON 20,000: RON 5,000 for medication and RON 15,000 for procedures. Separate local eligibility rules apply.

No remaining-place figures are published here. Check the official announcement and regulations. Local and national support cannot be combined in the same year: Sector 2 requires a declaration that the applicant has not received and is not receiving other public funding for the same purpose during the current year.

Sector 2 programme (RO) ↗

Approved by Sector 2 Local Council Decision No. 223/14 July 2026, the programme supports up to 50 beneficiaries a year. Applications must be submitted in person at the DGASPC Sector 2 registry, 15 Olari Street, Bucharest. Applicants must have lived in Sector 2 for at least one year, have no outstanding local tax debts and provide an ANAF income certificate. The participating clinics and pharmacies list is available on the DGASPC website.

Patient information and advocacy

SOS Infertilitatea provides information and advocates for access to support. Its website presents patient resources and initiatives; archived projects do not establish that applications are currently open.

Where to send documents

Proiect România provides information and links. It is not a clinic, funding administrator or announced programme partner. Do not send medical records, diagnoses, national identity numbers or identity documents through our contact form. Use the chosen programme's official channels.
